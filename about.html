About ZexMarketing

Senior-led marketing for the next wave of category leaders

We're a tight, specialist-led team that treats your growth goals like our own. No layers of account managers, no cookie-cutter playbooks.

Our Story

Built by marketers who missed doing the work

ZexMarketing was founded in 2019 after our team spent years at large agencies watching strategy decks get heavier while actual execution got thinner. We started with a simple belief: the best agency is the one that ships.

Today we partner with funded startups, growing ecommerce brands, and enterprise teams that need a senior bench without the enterprise overhead. Every engagement is led by someone who has done the work — not someone who just sells it.

🚀
What We Believe

Principles that guide every engagement

🎯

Outcomes over outputs

We measure ourselves by pipeline, revenue, and ROI — not the count of blog posts shipped or links placed.

🔬

Evidence over opinion

Every recommendation is grounded in data from your site, SERPs, and analytics — not trends from Twitter.

🤝

Partnership over vendor

We're embedded in your Slack, your planning, and your reviews. Your wins are our case studies.

📚

Teach as we ship

We document strategy and share frameworks so your team compounds learning — even after we part ways.

🛡️

White-hat, always

Sustainable tactics only. We've never had a client hit by an algorithm update for tactics we recommended.

⚡

Speed is a feature

Weekly shipping cadence, fast response windows, and tight feedback loops keep momentum high.
