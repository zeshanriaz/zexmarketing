Wildlife Conflicts Are Common in LA

LA's mix of hillside terrain, mature trees, and open space near residential neighborhoods means wildlife conflicts come up often — raccoons and squirrels in attics, opossums and skunks under decks or crawlspaces. These situations call for humane trapping and exclusion rather than general pest treatment, since the goal is to remove the animal safely and keep it from getting back in.

Signs You Have a Wildlife Problem

  • Heavy footsteps or scratching in the attic at night (raccoons, squirrels)
  • A strong odor from under a deck or porch (skunks, opossums)
  • Torn vent screens, damaged roofline, or gaps around utility lines
  • Droppings in the attic or garage
  • A visible den or nesting material
  • Unusual pet behavior — barking or pawing at a wall, deck, or crawlspace opening

Our Wildlife Removal Process

1

Inspection

We identify the species, entry points, and extent of any attic or crawlspace damage.

2

Humane Trapping & Exclusion

We trap in compliance with California wildlife regulations and use one-way exclusion doors that let animals leave without re-entering.

3

Entry Point Sealing

Once the animal is confirmed gone, we close off roofline gaps, vents, and other access points.

4

Cleanup & Sanitation

We remove droppings, damaged insulation, and nesting material, and sanitize affected areas.

Which Animals We Handle

We handle common nuisance wildlife including raccoons, opossums, squirrels, and skunks. Larger wildlife concerns such as coyotes typically fall under city or county animal control rather than pest control, and we can point homeowners to the right resource if that's the situation.
