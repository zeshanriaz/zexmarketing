Cockroach Control in Los Angeles

German cockroaches are the most common indoor roach problem in LA kitchens and apartments, reproducing quickly and spreading between units in multi-family buildings. American cockroaches, often called "waterbugs," are larger and typically come up through drains, sewer connections, and damp areas.

Cockroaches Are a Persistent Problem in LA Kitchens

German cockroaches thrive in the warm, humid conditions found around kitchen appliances and bathrooms, and their fast reproduction cycle means a small problem can become a large one within weeks. In apartment buildings, they move easily between units through shared plumbing and wall voids, so a single unit's infestation can quickly become a building-wide issue. American cockroaches behave differently, favoring drains, sewer lines, and damp crawlspaces, and often showing up after entering through pipes or gaps near plumbing fixtures.

Signs You Have a Cockroach Problem

  • Droppings resembling coffee grounds or black pepper near food storage and appliances
  • A musty, oily odor in heavily infested areas
  • Egg cases (oothecae) tucked into cracks and crevices
  • Roaches seen scurrying when a light is switched on at night
  • Roaches near drains or plumbing, typically a sign of American roaches

Our Cockroach Control Process

1

Inspection

We identify the species involved and locate hot spots — behind appliances, under sinks, and along drain lines.

2

Gel Baiting & IGR

Gel bait is placed into cracks and crevices along with an insect growth regulator (IGR) to break the reproduction cycle.

3

Sanitation Guidance

We provide recommendations on food storage, trash management, and drain maintenance that help starve out remaining roaches.

4

Follow-Up Visits

German roach infestations typically need two to three visits over several weeks to fully break the reproduction cycle.

Why Foggers & Bombs Don't Work

Foggers only kill roaches caught out in the open at the moment they're used, and they don't reach into the cracks, voids, and wall spaces where roaches actually live and breed. In apartment buildings, foggers can even scatter roaches into neighboring units, making the overall problem worse rather than better. Gel baiting and IGR treatment target roaches where they hide and interrupt the reproduction cycle directly.
