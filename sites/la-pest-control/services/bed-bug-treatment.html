Bed Bugs Are a Growing Problem in LA Housing

Bed bugs are expert hitchhikers, moving in on luggage, secondhand furniture, and clothing before spreading through shared walls, hallways, and laundry rooms. In apartment buildings and hotels, a single infestation can quietly travel to neighboring units before anyone notices. Because they hide deep in seams and cracks and only feed while people sleep, bed bugs are often well established by the time they're discovered — and general pest sprays used for other insects simply aren't built to reach or kill them.

Signs You Have Bed Bugs

  • Small reddish-brown bugs in mattress seams, box springs, or headboard cracks
  • Itchy bites often appearing in a line or cluster on the skin
  • Tiny rust-colored spots on sheets (fecal marks) or small blood spots
  • Pale, shed exoskeletons near mattress seams and other hiding spots
  • A musty, sweet odor in heavily infested rooms
  • Bugs found in furniture seams, behind headboards, or along baseboards near the bed

Our Bed Bug Treatment Process

1

Inspection

We thoroughly check the mattress, furniture seams, baseboards, and — where relevant — adjoining units for signs of activity.

2

Treatment Plan

Depending on the property, we recommend heat treatment, which raises room temperature to kill bugs and eggs often in a single visit, or a targeted chemical treatment across multiple visits.

3

Preparation Guidance

We provide clear instructions on laundering, bagging items, and decluttering so the treatment can work effectively.

4

Follow-Up Visit

We confirm bed bugs are eliminated at every life stage, since eggs can survive an initial treatment and hatch afterward.

Apartments & Multi-Unit Buildings

Bed bugs can travel between adjoining units through wall voids and shared plumbing lines, which means fully resolving an infestation in an apartment often requires inspecting neighboring units as well. We work directly with property managers and landlords on multi-unit treatment protocols to help stop bed bugs from spreading building-wide.
