Mosquitoes Don't Need Much to Breed

Mosquitoes can breed in even small amounts of standing water — a clogged gutter, a saucer under a potted plant, or a low spot in the yard is enough. In Los Angeles, mosquito activity typically increases in the warmer months and is worst around dusk and dawn, making evenings on the patio a lot less enjoyable without treatment.

Signs of a Mosquito Problem

  • Bites appearing mostly in the evening or early morning
  • Standing water sources on the property — drainage areas, birdbaths, unused planters
  • Visible mosquito activity in shaded, humid areas of the yard
  • Noticeable buzzing near patios or outdoor seating at dusk
  • Increased bites after rain or irrigation runoff pools in the yard
