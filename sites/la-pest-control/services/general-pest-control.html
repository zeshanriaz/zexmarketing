Why General Pest Control Matters in LA

Los Angeles' mild, dry climate means pests never fully go dormant the way they do in colder regions. Ants, spiders, and occasional roaches are active nearly year-round, and older housing stock throughout the city — with its stucco cracks, weep holes, and aging weatherstripping — gives them easy access indoors. A single seasonal spray rarely holds; ongoing, scheduled service is what actually keeps pest pressure down.

Signs You Need General Pest Control

  • Ant trails appearing in the kitchen or bathroom, especially after watering the yard
  • Spider webs building up in corners, eaves, or the garage faster than you can clear them
  • Occasional roach or silverfish sightings, particularly in damp areas
  • Small pests appearing after rain or a change in weather
  • Previous DIY sprays that worked briefly, then stopped

Our General Pest Control Process

General pest control is built around routine, scheduled service rather than one-off spraying:

1

Inspection

We walk the interior and exterior to identify current activity and conducive conditions.

2

Exterior Barrier

A perimeter treatment around the foundation, eaves, and entry points reduces pests before they get inside.

3

Interior Spot Treatment

Targeted treatment of kitchens, bathrooms, and any active problem areas.

4

Ongoing Visits

Recurring bi-monthly or quarterly service keeps protection in place as seasons change.
