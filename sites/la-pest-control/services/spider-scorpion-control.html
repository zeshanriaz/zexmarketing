Spiders and Scorpions in Los Angeles

Most spiders found around LA homes are harmless and even help keep other pests in check. Black widows are the exception — they're common in garages, woodpiles, and undisturbed corners, and brown widows have become increasingly common across Southern California as well. Scorpions tend to show up more often in hillside and foothill-adjacent neighborhoods near open space, where rocky terrain and dry landscaping give them plenty of places to hide.

Signs You Have a Spider or Scorpion Problem

  • Webs building up in garages, eaves, woodpiles, or storage areas faster than usual
  • Sightings of black or brown widow spiders in dark, undisturbed corners
  • Scorpions found in shoes, under furniture, or spotted glowing under a UV/blacklight at night
  • Increased activity after clearing yard debris or moving stored items
  • Egg sacs tucked into corners of garages, sheds, or crawlspaces
  • New sightings after landscaping work near rock walls or woodpiles

Are They Dangerous?

Black widow and brown widow bites, along with scorpion stings, are rarely life-threatening for healthy adults, but they can cause significant pain and should be treated as a medical concern — especially for children, older adults, or anyone with an allergic reaction. Prompt treatment around the property reduces the chance of an encounter in the first place.
