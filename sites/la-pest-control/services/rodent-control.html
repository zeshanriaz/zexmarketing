Rodents Are a Common Problem in LA Homes

Roof rats are the most frequent rodent issue we see in Los Angeles — they're strong climbers, nesting in attics, trees, and along power lines, and they can squeeze through gaps as small as a quarter. Norway rats and house mice show up too, particularly around crawlspaces, cluttered garages, and properties near sewer lines. Older rooflines, unsealed vents, and gaps around plumbing give rodents an easy way into a structure.

Signs You Have a Rodent Problem

  • Droppings near food storage, along baseboards, or in the attic
  • Gnaw marks on wood, wiring, or plastic storage containers
  • Scratching or scurrying sounds in the attic or walls, especially at night
  • Greasy rub marks along beams and baseboards where rodents travel repeatedly
  • A strong, ammonia-like odor in enclosed spaces
  • Shredded paper or insulation used as nesting material in attics or garages

Roof Rats vs. Norway Rats vs. House Mice

Roof rats nest above ground — attics, trees, eaves, and dense vegetation. Norway rats burrow at ground level, often near foundations, crawlspaces, and sewer connections. House mice are smaller and tend to nest in wall voids, cluttered garages, and storage areas. Correctly identifying which one you have changes where we focus trapping and exclusion.
