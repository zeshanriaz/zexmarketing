About Los Angeles Exterminators

A locally focused pest control company built around fast response times, honest pricing, and treatments that are safe for the whole household.

Our Approach

Los Angeles Exterminators was built on a simple idea: pest control should be fast, transparent, and effective — without the upsells, long-term contracts, or vague pricing that homeowners often run into. We serve single-family homes, apartments, and commercial properties across Los Angeles County, and every visit starts with a real inspection, not a guess.

Southern California's mild climate keeps pests active year-round, and Los Angeles' mix of older housing stock, dense multi-family buildings, and hillside terrain means the right treatment plan looks different from block to block. Our technicians are trained to identify not just the pest, but the conditions letting it thrive — moisture, entry points, harborage, and food sources — so treatments actually hold.

Licensing & Standards

Pest control operators in California are required to be licensed through the Structural Pest Control Board, a branch of the California Department of Consumer Affairs. Los Angeles Exterminators operates in compliance with state licensing and insurance requirements, and our full license details are available upon request or by phone.
