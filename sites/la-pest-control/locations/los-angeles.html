Pest Control Across the City of Los Angeles

The City of Los Angeles covers a huge range of housing types and terrain, and pest pressure looks different block to block. Dense apartment corridors in Koreatown and Downtown LA see more roach and rodent pressure from shared walls and trash areas. Older craftsman and bungalow neighborhoods like Los Feliz, Eagle Rock, and Mid-City tend to see more ants, spiders, and termite activity in original wood framing. Neighborhoods bordering hillside parkland, like areas near Griffith Park, also see more wildlife and rodent activity migrating in from open space.

Common Pests in Los Angeles

  • Argentine ants trailing indoors, especially after watering or rain
  • German cockroaches in apartment buildings and older multi-family housing
  • Roof rats in attics, trees, and rooflines throughout residential neighborhoods
  • Drywood and subterranean termites in pre-1960s wood-frame homes
  • Spiders and occasional black widows in garages and undisturbed corners
