Pest Control in Pasadena

Pasadena's historic craftsman bungalow neighborhoods, many built in the early 1900s through the 1930s, carry a higher termite risk due to original untreated wood framing — this is one of the most termite-active areas we serve. The city's foothill-adjacent location near the San Gabriel Mountains also brings more ant activity and occasional black widow sightings in older detached garages.

Common Pests in Pasadena

  • Drywood termites in historic wood-frame homes
  • Subterranean termites near foundations and older plumbing
  • Argentine ants, especially after irrigation or rain
  • Black widow spiders in older garages and storage areas
  • Occasional rodent activity in the attics of older homes
