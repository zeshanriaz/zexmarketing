      How to use it

      1. Type or paste your entries in the box, one per line.
      2. Click the wheel or press Ctrl+Enter.
      3. Remove the winner, spin again, or keep going.
      4. Save the wheel or share it with a link.

      FAQ

      Is the wheel spinner random?

      Yes. ZexWheel uses crypto.getRandomValues to pick the winner, then lands the wheel on that slice at a random spot inside it.

      How do I give one entry more chances?

      Add an asterisk and a number after the name. Bob*2 gets twice the odds of an entry with no weight.

      Where are my wheels stored?

      In your own browser storage. Nothing leaves your device unless you share a link.

      Does it work offline?

      Yes. After one visit the app caches itself. Use your browser's Install option to add it to your home screen.

      What keyboard shortcuts exist?

      Ctrl+Enter spins. Space spins when the text box is not focused. F toggles fullscreen. Esc closes pop ups.
