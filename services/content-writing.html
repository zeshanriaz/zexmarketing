Our Editorial Process

A 6-step workflow that keeps quality high

Every piece we publish follows the same blueprint. No drive-by drafts, no copy-paste jobs.

  • 1SEO brief — keyword target, SERP analysis, outline, and angle locked before anyone writes a word.
  • 2Subject-matter match — we assign a writer with direct experience in your domain.
  • 3Research & interviews — primary sources, expert quotes, and original data where possible.
  • 4Draft & self-edit — our writers don't submit first drafts; everything is reviewed and tightened.
  • 5Editorial review — a senior editor polishes voice, structure, and SEO execution.
  • 6Your review & publish — you get a clean draft; two rounds of revisions included.
